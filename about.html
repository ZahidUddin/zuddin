Backend craftsmanship built on deep fundamentals.

8+ years of commercial software engineering, scaling WordPress & Shopify architectures, crafting high-performance PHP APIs, and building maintainable digital platforms.

Hi, I'm Zahid Uddin - a software engineer passionate about clean code, high-scale CMS architecture, and high-converting e-commerce.

Over the past 8 years, I have built and maintained 35+ commercial websites, bespoke Shopify stores, and enterprise WordPress platforms for clients across the United States, Europe, and Asia. My expertise centers on translating complex business logic into clean, robust object-oriented PHP, custom Full Site Editing (FSE) themes, bespoke Gutenberg blocks with ACF Pro, tailor-made Shopify Liquid sections, and scalable REST APIs.

I reject the bloated "page-builder sprawl" common in traditional web development. Instead, I write modular, zero-dependency code, optimize database indexes, configure Redis object caching, and fine-tune server stacks (PHP-FPM, OPcache, NGINX, Cloudflare) to deliver sub-second page loads and 95+ Core Web Vitals scores across both WordPress and Shopify ecosystems.
